Rules — encryption without the words (M6)

What is private (D-11), and what people see

ConversationEnd-to-endWhat people see
DMs and group DMs✓  all of them: new ones from the start, existing ones switch once at M6 (can’t be undone)Nothing new. No lock, no badge, no notice. The first time they open one on a new device, they confirm that device.
Private channels✓The lock, which still only means private.
Public workspace channels—Unchanged: newcomers see earlier history (D-16), and search works on the server.
Client-space channels—Unchanged. A client opens the join link and is in; they meet the confirm step only when they first open a DM.
Never—The words “encrypted” or “not encrypted”, a shield, a warning colour (D-11). The “encryption turned on” event is hidden in our timeline; Element X shows its own.

Words

keys, cross-signing, session, verifyConfirm this device · Not confirmed“Device” is already the word in Settings → Devices. “Confirm” is what the person does; nothing to learn.
security key, passphraseRecovery key / リカバリーキーKept on purpose: it is the one thing people must store outside the app, so it needs a name they can find again in a password manager. Element X uses the same name, so help from either app matches. “Key” never appears alone.
unable to decrypt, encrypted messageNot available on this deviceTrue for every cause (before a reset, a key that never came), and it says where to look: another device may still show it.
identity resetReset and start fresh?Says what the person chooses; the two rows under it say what it costs.
Emoji namesFrom the Matrix emoji table (EN/JP)Both devices, and Element X, show the same names.

Messages this device can’t open

RowSender, time and one quiet line with a dashed bubble: Not available on this device. A run from one sender collapses into 3 messages not available on this device. Same words in threads and as a DM list preview. No hover bar, no lock, no colour.
Still arrivingA shimmer line for up to 10 s while a key can still come (backup, the other device); then the quiet line. If it arrives later, the message fades in where it is (180 ms).
Before you joinedPrivate channels don’t show messages from before you joined at all; the timeline starts at your join row.
Gate, not rowsAn unconfirmed device gets the confirm card instead of a page of quiet rows: other apps (Element X) no longer send to unconfirmed devices, so the rows would never fill in.
