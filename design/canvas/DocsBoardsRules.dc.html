Where they live

RuleBoards
ScopeThe selected space tile decides what Docs and Boards list. A client space tile: only that client space. The Pasodrum tile: the workspace plus every client space you are in, one group each, the client ones with the outside mark.DocsPage, DocsNordlys, BoardsNordlys
ClientsSee, create, edit and comment on pages and issues in their own client space; can be assigned issues. They can’t add columns. The server keeps everything else out (D-06). No outside mark, no Translate.DocsClient, BoardsClient
Not per channelPages and projects belong to a space. Channel tabs for them may come later; not decided.—
RemovedDeleting the Docs or Boards feature folder: the rail item goes, links to pages or issues show the existing not available page, the rooms stay hidden and intact (D-04).NotAvailable

Docs

RuleBoards
PagesTitle, blocks, child pages. The sidebar tree nests them per space; drag to move. New page: the pencil in the sidebar, or a child page from a block.DocsEmpty
BlocksThe + beside a line opens the block list (typing / opens the same list). Text, headings, lists, checklist, quote, callout, code, table, image or file, link to a page, divider.DocsBlocks
Links[[ or Link to a page searches pages in the same space, or makes a new one. Linked from at the end lists pages that link here.DocsLink
TogetherAvatars of who is on the page. 森 真由 is typing… in the header. Each person’s caret and selection in their avatar colour with a name flag.DocsPage, DocsPageJP
SavingNo save button and no Saved. Offline: the Offline band, editing goes on, changes merge when back; other people’s carets go.DocsOffline
UntitledA new page is Untitled until it has a title. Nothing else on an empty page but the +.DocsEmpty
No pagesA space with no pages: No pages yet and New page. Nothing else.DocsNone(+JP)
LoadingGrey skeleton of a title and lines, shimmering (D-20); reduced motion: still.DocsLoading
Page menuMore in the header: Copy link, Move to…, Delete page. Move to lists the same space only; the current place is greyed. Delete asks once, with the page in the box; pages inside it go too.DocsPageMenu(+JP/Dark), DocsMoveTo, DocsDeletePage
DraggingBlocks by their grip: the block dims, a copy follows, an accent line shows where it lands. Pages in the tree: a line indented to its new depth, or the row highlighted to put it inside.DocsDragBlock, DocsDragPage

Boards

RuleBoards
ProjectsA project has a name and a colour and belongs to one space. Issues get short ids from the project (WEB-18).BoardsPage
ColumnsNew projects start with To do In progress In review Done, named by the app in each language. + after the last column adds one (team only). A custom column has one title, shown as typed: a Japanese client’s board simply gets Japanese titles. Done dims its issues and keeps them out of My issues.BoardsColumn(+JP), BoardsNordlys
IssuesTitle, description (same blocks as Docs), column, assignee, due date, comments. New issue asks only for a title; the rest are chips. Quick add: the faint + under a column’s last card opens a card with just a title; Enter creates it there.BoardsNewIssue, BoardsQuickAdd
ViewsBoard (drag between columns) or List, remembered per project. Filter by person in the header.BoardsPage, BoardsList
My issuesEverything assigned to you, grouped by project, done left out. In a client space tile: that client space only.BoardsMine
New projectName and colour; it lands in the space whose + was used. No projects yet: No projects yet and New project. A project with no issues: empty columns with their faint +.BoardsNewProject, BoardsNoProjects(+JP), BoardsEmpty
Card menuRight-click or More on a card: Open, Move to (columns), Assign, Copy link, Delete. This is also the keyboard way to move. Delete asks once, with the issue in the box.BoardsCardMenu(+JP/Dark), BoardsDeleteIssue
DraggingYour card lifts, tilts and follows; an accent line with a ring shows where it lands and the column tints. Not in List or My issues.BoardsDrag
LoadingSkeleton cards in each column, shimmering.BoardsLoading
PanelAn issue opens beside the board, like a thread. Column, assignee and due are one click each. Overdue dates are red.BoardsIssue, BoardsAssignee, BoardsDue
TogetherLight multiplayer, in each person’s avatar colour with a name flag, like carets in Docs: a card someone is dragging lifts a little in place with a slight tilt (animated up, 180 ms) and their flag, and their drop line shows where it will go (the card itself doesn’t travel); a card someone is editing gets their outline; a card someone just moved glows for a moment. In an open issue: who else has it open (avatar in the panel header) and Jonas Berg is typing… above the comment box.BoardsLive, BoardsLiveIssue
FailedA move that didn’t save: the card goes back and a toast says Couldn’t move WEB-21. In a dialog: a red line Couldn’t create the issue. Try again. above the buttons (same as the rest of the app).BoardsMoveFailed, BoardsCreateFailed
CommentsSame as chat: reactions, @mentions, edit, delete. Changes to column, assignee and due show between them as small grey one-liners with a dot, no avatar or bold, so comments stand out.BoardsIssue

Translate

RuleBoards
WhoTeam only (D-32). Never in a client space, client-space pages and projects included (CEO).—
DocsOne section at a time: a heading and what’s under it, or the title and intro. Hover a section in the other language and Translate shows beside its first line; the section then reads in your language with Show original under its heading. No whole-page translation. Editing a translated section shows the original.DocsTranslated
StatesWhile a section translates its text pulses and the line shows the glyph and three dots. Failed: a red glyph, Couldn’t translate · Retry; the original stays readable. Japanese readers translate English sections the same way.DocsTranslateStates, DocsTranslatedJP(+Dark)
BoardsTranslate on a comment’s hover bar, then Show original, as in chat; issue title and description the same way. A card whose title is in the other language shows Translate on hover; once translated, a small glyph stays on the card and brings back the original.BoardsIssue, BoardsCardTranslate
