Client and External marks: everywhere they show

Sizes
Avatar corner 14 px on avatars of 30 px and up, top-right, inset −4 px, 2 px ring in the surface colour. Nothing below 30 px. The line’s marks are 18 px, up to three, then the text.
↗ mark
Circle on a 14-unit grid; path M4.6 9.4 9.4 4.6 M5.8 4.6h3.6v3.6, stroke 1.6, round caps. Light #55535C with white; dark #B3B1BC with #141418.
Company tile
Space colour, first letter in Inter 700 at 62 % of the size, radius ¼ of the size; the space’s logo when set. Dark mode adds a 1 px inner light edge.
The line
Light #FBF0DA, border #EFDDB4, text #3A2A00. Dark #3D2A10, border #5A3F17, text #F3E6CF. 14 px, company names bold; joined to the composer’s top edge.
