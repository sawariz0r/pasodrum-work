App window — title bar

Our own title bar, in the rail colour, across the topNEW

Mac keeps the OS traffic lights, inset. Windows gets our drawn controls. Below the bar nothing changes. Before sign-in the bar is empty.

Real size

40 px, the rail’s colour. ← → go back and forward (the app has no browser buttons). The search field opens the approved switcher (⌘K / Ctrl K still work); in the app it replaces the header’s search icon. Empty space drags the window; double-click zooms (Mac) or maximises (Windows).
