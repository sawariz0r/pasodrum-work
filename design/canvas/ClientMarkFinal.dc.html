Client and External marks

N Clients
Their company on the avatar corner: the client space’s tile, or its logo when one is set. A coloured rounded square.
Externals
A grey circle with ↗. Round and grey, so it never reads as a company.
Team
Nothing.
The line above the composer
Shows when someone in the conversation doesn’t belong to the space you’re in: Externals and guests anywhere, clients in DMs. Never for a client space’s own clients in their space. Thread replies too. Clicking it opens Members in channels.
Unchanged
Rail, sidebar, chat lists and the header stay as they are. What goes: the yellow building on rail tiles, sidebar rows, after channel and DM names, beside Send, and on space tiles; the amber ↗ corner.
Who sees it
The team and Externals, as today. Clients never see corners or the line.
