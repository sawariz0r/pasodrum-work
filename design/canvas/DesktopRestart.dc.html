Restart: working and failed

Update card · Restart pressed

Shows until the app quits; the macOS password prompt may come first. A second press does nothing. If the install fails, the button goes back to Restart and the alert below opens.

Restart failed · macOS alert

The OS’s own alert, no detail line (the error goes to the app’s log). Download opens pasodrum.work/download/mac in the browser; Later closes it and the card stays, so Restart can be tried again. Same alert from the app menu’s Restart to Update.
