Server (only what it can do)

Renamem.room.name only. The room ID never changes, so links, threads and other apps keep working. The taken check runs over channels in this space I can see (as New channel) and again just before saving.
ArchiveAs D-28 per channel: events_default, m.reaction and m.room.redaction to 50, plus work.pasodrum.channel {archived: true} so every client knows to drop it from the sidebar and show the bar. Unarchive restores the D-27 levels and removes the flag. Element X: can’t post (the server refuses), shows the channel as before.
Known gapD-28 again: the creator outranks the levels, so a Member who made the channel can still post after archiving. The bar shows to everyone anyway.
Client spacesSame screens. Where someone added to a client space lands skips archived channels. An archived client space (D-28) stays as it is: its channels keep their sidebar.
