Kept apart by the server

  • Membership is the access. A role is our record. Saving it invites the person to the rooms it covers and removes them from the rest. The server refuses everything else.
  • Externals aren’t in the workspace, so they can never join a workspace channel they weren’t invited to.
  • Clients see only their client space: its Staff, the Externals given that space, and their own colleagues. Never another client, never another client space, never role names.
  • An External on two client spaces doesn’t join them up. Auto-accept gets stricter: a room from outside my spaces waits unanswered.
  • Externals add nobody and create nothing: adding people and creating channels need level 10.
  • Removing someone takes them out of the space first, then every channel, and kills their join links.

Agreed with the CEO (2026-11-21)

  1. Owner, Admin and Staff all stay; Admins have Owner-like rights.
  2. Guests become Externals. Nobody’s access changes.
  3. One-off channels for Externals as well as roles.
  4. Owners and Admins add Externals and run roles.
  5. Clients see Externals as part of the team, with no tag or mark. The team sees the mark and the External tag.
  6. A client space in a role is all or nothing, for now.
  7. Words: Staff / External / Client, “Access”, “roles”. JP: スタッフ / 外部 / クライアント, アクセス, ロール.
  8. Staff are trusted: the server doesn’t stop them adding existing accounts.
