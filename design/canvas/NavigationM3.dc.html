Rules

Rail vs sidebarThe sidebar shows the workspace you’re in. The rail shows everything: DMs and Activity span the workspace and every client space.
ThreadsThreads you started, replied in or were @mentioned in. Unread first; the order holds while the view is open. A card opens the thread panel and marks it read. Sidebar badge = threads with unread replies.
Activity@mentions (threads included) and replies to your messages. All · Mentions · Replies. Rail badge = unread items. A row opens the message in its channel or thread.
DraftsThis device only (D-16). Count in quiet grey, never red. A row opens the conversation with the draft in the composer; Delete on hover.
DMsRail page: all DMs with the last line and time. Sidebar: DMs with people in this workspace. Client people carry the amber corner. A new DM joins the sidebar when opened.
New messageOne dialog for a DM and a group DM. Enter adds a person; Message opens the conversation. An existing DM opens instead of a duplicate.
Profile cardClick a name or avatar: name, local time with city (from the profile time zone; hidden when unknown), work email, Message. Never an ID.
Thread panelFirst reply: composer focused. Loading: skeleton under the root. Can’t load: one line + Retry; the composer still works. Same composer as the channel; no “Also send to channel”.
ClientsDMs and New message list only people in their client space; the server’s directory decides, not the UI.
