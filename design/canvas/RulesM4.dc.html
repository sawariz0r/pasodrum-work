Behaviour

RolesOwner = PL 100, Admin = PL 50 in the workspace (D-06). Everyone sees Owner / Admin / Guest / Client tags; Member has no tag. Owners and Admins see the role switch on a profile card (D-25): Admins pick Member or Admin; Owners also make and remove Owners, Owners included. “Owner” asks once because it hands over full control. Nobody switches their own role; clients and guests have none.
ChannelsNew channel: space (current one preselected; only spaces where you may create: a client space’s own team, Owners and Admins, never clients), name (lowercase, spaces become hyphens, 80 max, unique per space), Private. The name’s # turns into the lock when Private is on. Privacy is fixed after creation. A lock means private; no encryption copy anywhere.
LeavePublic: leave at once (Browse brings you back). Private: asks first, because only a member can add you again.
SettingsChanges save as you make them. Time zone is set from the device at first sign-in and drives local time on the profile card. Email is read-only. Devices: this device first; Sign out per device and for all others.
ComposerEvery new board: the composer floats over the timeline and the timeline never scrolls sideways (Foundations → Messages).
First runOpens the channel you were added to, scrolled to the latest, earlier history visible (D-16). Everything before you joined counts as read: no badges, no Activity count, empty DMs. Name comes from the email. No tour, no helper text; the notification ask waits for the first send.
