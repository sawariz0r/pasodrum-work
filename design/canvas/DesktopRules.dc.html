Desktop app — rules

WindowOur own 40 px title bar in the rail colour: ← →, the search field, Mac traffic lights inset, Windows controls drawn by us. One window; it opens where it was left (first launch 1280 × 800). Smallest 800 × 600, so Window800 and Window1024 layouts apply.
ClosingMac: closing the window keeps the app in the Dock; ⌘Q quits. Windows: closing hides it to the tray; Quit is in the tray menu. Notifications and the badge keep working.
Signing inSame sign-in and same device confirmation as a new browser. The app is its own device: “Pasodrum work on Mac” / “Pasodrum work on Windows” (JP “Mac の Pasodrum work”).
LinksLinks to pasodrum.work inside the app open in the app. Every other link opens in the default browser. Links opened outside the app (email, join links) open in the browser. No hand-off page.
BadgeUnread mentions + DMs, the same things that notify. Nothing unread for you → no badge. Caps at 99+. Mac: Dock. Windows: taskbar overlay, plus a dot on the tray icon.
NotificationsAs approved (DesktopNotification). Clicking one brings the window forward at that message. The NotifyAsk card stays as it is.
UpdatesDownloaded in the background, installed on quit. When one is ready: the “Update ready” card in the sidebar (NotifyAsk’s slot, one card at a time) and “Restart to Update” in the Mac app menu. × hides the card until the next launch.
Can’t reachOnly when the window can’t load the app at all: “Can’t reach Pasodrum work” + Try again (also retries by itself). Once loaded, the approved Offline / Reconnecting / Back online states apply.
Download appOnly in a browser on Mac or Windows: profile menu, join page header, join done. The label names the OS. Never in the app, on phones or on Linux. Goes to pasodrum.work/download/mac or /windows.
Shell-neutralEverything here works in Tauri and Electron. Only platform/ touches the shell: notify, setBadge, openExternal, onOpenRoute, deviceName, plus update and tray.
