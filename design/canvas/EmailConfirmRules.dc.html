Rules — confirm by email (clients)

Who gets what

ClientsConfirm a new device by email. Never see a recovery key: it is made silently on their first DM and kept, locked, by our server (invite service, apart from messages). Settings → Devices has no Recovery key row for them.
Staff and the teamUnchanged (RulesM6): another device or the recovery key. Our server never holds their key.
Another device signed inClients also get Use another device (the existing emoji step) under Email me a link.

When each screen appears

New deviceOpening a DM shows Confirm it’s you where the conversation would be (same place as the M6 gate). One line says why, and says sorry for the extra step.
Email me a linkSends the email to the address on their profile and shows Check your email with the address and a quiet pulse. The page waits; nothing to type.
The linkOpens a page that works on any device, phone included (not the phone hand-off). It asks Confirm this device? with the device name and time, because mail scanners open links on their own: only the tap confirms. There is no “It wasn’t me”: ignoring the email is enough, the link dies after 15 minutes. A worried client taps the help pill.
Yes, it’s meThe phone shows Confirmed. The waiting device shows You’re all set (closes after 1.2 s); the conversation and older messages fade in.
Didn’t get it?Send it again (at most once a minute) and Ask Jonas Berg to help me. Start fresh opens the existing reset; staff confirm them on a call and share history back (SIDE-03).
Link rulesWorks once, for 15 minutes, only for the device that asked. A new email replaces the old link. Expired or used: This link no longer works.

Help me (on every screen where something can go wrong)

WhereThe same pill, Need help? Ask Jonas Berg, with the contact’s face: on Confirm it’s you, Check your email, the link page (confirm, no longer works). The Didn’t-get-it card shows it as the main row. Never on success screens.
Who is the contactThe team person who added the client to their client space. If they’ve left, the space’s Owners and Admins. The client sees one name.
What happensOne tap, nothing to type. The client sees Jonas Berg will help you. Jonas gets a DM from Pasodrum (our system sender, read-only): who, which client space, what went wrong, their email and local time, Email Henrik and View profile. A desktop notification as for any DM.
What went wrongFilled in from where they tapped: email didn’t arrive · stuck confirming · link didn’t work.
LimitsOnly a signed-in client, or one holding a valid link, can send it; at most once every 10 minutes (the button then says it’s already sent). The message never carries anything secret.
Other appsThe message is plain text with a full body, so Element X shows it too (D-05).

Words

We sayConfirm it’s you · Email me a link · Check your email · You’re all set
Neverkey, recovery key (for clients), verify, encryption, security code. No warning colours on the client screens.
ToneAdmit it’s a bit of a hassle once, say why in plain words, then get out of the way. No more lines than the boards show.

What we accept (CEO, 2026-11-21)

EmailWhoever controls the client’s mailbox can confirm a device. Same as a password reset today.
Our serverKeeps each client’s key so it can send the link any time. Someone who breaks into our server could read client DMs, the same as client-space channels today (D-11). Staff conversations without clients are not affected.
Element XClients using Element X keep its own confirm steps (emoji, or recovery key). We don’t show clients the key, so for them Element X is chat only after they’ve confirmed in our app.
