Room left for what’s next

Chat, threads, client spaces and Add people are standard Matrix that a Continuwuity server handles today. Docs and boards keep their place in the layout for later. Calls are out for now because they need a separate LiveKit server.

Docs

Rich multiplayer pages. They live in the rail’s Docs section and as a Docs tab on every channel, so a doc sits right next to the conversation about it.

Boards

Kanban boards in the rail’s Boards section, and as a channel tab. Clients see the board in their client space and nothing else that’s internal.

Client spaces

Their own tile on the rail, marked with the amber building, and a Client role in Add people. One look tells you a conversation leaves the company.
