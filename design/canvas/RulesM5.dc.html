Client spaces (Owners and Admins)

NewRail + or sidebar +, Owners and Admins only (Members never see it). Create shows the three-dot pulse while the space, its channels and the client emails are made; the fields rest at 60 %. Name taken (any space, case-insensitive): red ring, one line, Create off. Failure: one line in the footer, Create becomes Retry; nothing half-made is left behind. Done: you land in its first channel, the tile pops into the rail with the white ring.
SettingsSpace menu → Client space settings. General: name, colour (saves as you change it), Archive. People: the team on top, the client below, pending people with the clock.
Remove a personAsked once, showing the channels they leave. They leave the space and every channel in it at once; a pending person’s join link stops working. Their messages stay, under their name. DMs are not touched. The account is never deleted: adding the same email later brings the same person back.
ArchiveAsked once. Every channel turns read-only for everyone; nobody is removed, so the client keeps their history. The composer becomes one quiet bar; the tile dims and moves to the end of the rail. Owners and Admins can unarchive from that bar.

Another app and phones (D-15)

Another appProfile menu → Another app: server and username with Copy (check for 1.2 s, announced), and Get Element X. The only place a server name appears. The join page’s done state keeps the same pair behind its disclosure.
UsernameThe account’s own ID part (D-07): sam.okafor for the team, henrik_nordlys.no for a client.
PhonesThe app is desktop-width. An app link opened on a phone shows one calm page: what it points to, Copy link, Open in Element X. The join page itself works on phones; after joining there, Element X comes first and the server and username are shown open.

D-25 in one line

Owners make and remove Owners. Owners and Admins switch people between Member and Admin. Channels in a client space: Owners, Admins and that space’s team; never clients. Boards: RoleChange (Owner on an Owner), RoleChangeAdmin, MakeOwner, RulesM4.
